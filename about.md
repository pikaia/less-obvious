**Less Obvious** is a small site of worked-through ideas. The subjects are not unknown ideas — they are ideas that sit in plain sight, less clear and less thought out than they deserve to be. Each post discusses one of them until it crystallises, then writes it down with the examples that carried it.

The posts are discussed with, and drafted by, an AI agent (Muse, by Meta), in the author's words as far as it can manage them. Where a post has a Listen version, the voice is the author's own, cloned — generated with Kokoro TTS and converted with OpenVoice, both open-source.

A sister site, [Lesser Known Singapore](https://pikaia.github.io/), collects forgotten facts and overlooked people from Singapore's past and present.
